Salon Queue - Brief
Problem

On weekends, customers wait 1-2 hours in the salon without knowing when their turn will come. Some leave in frustration, and others argue about who arrived first. The salon has no fair, visible system for the order of service.

Users
Customer: joins the queue from a phone, tracks position and wait, gets alerted when called.
Receptionist: adds walk-ins, calls the next customer, handles no-shows and cancellations.
Barber: sees the current customer, taps Start / Done, marks break.
Owner: manages services, barbers on duty, opens/closes the online queue, sees daily stats.
Must-have features
Join the queue from your phone: enter name and phone, pick up to 3 services and a barber (or "Any barber").
Live status: token number, position in line, estimated wait, "Start coming" alert and "It's your turn!" alert.
Receptionist dashboard: add walk-ins, call next per barber, mark no-show, cancel.
Barber screen: current customer, Start / Done buttons, On break / Available toggle.
Waiting-area TV: current token per barber and the next 5 tokens.
Owner settings: services (name, minutes, price, on/off), barbers on duty, open/close online queue, today's stats.
English and Gujarati labels with a language toggle.
Fair queue rules: first come first served, with barber preferences respected.
Don't build
Online payment
Future-date appointments
SMS / WhatsApp notifications
Loyalty points or discounts
Multiple branches
Customer accounts / passwords (customers are identified by phone number only)
Look & feel

Warm, elegant salon feel: light theme with cream background (#FFF7ED) and burgundy accent (#7F1D1D), white rounded cards, clean and mobile-first. Labels in English and Gujarati.
